03

There is always a better one

The same coat at each rarity. Higher tier = a warmer frame, a bigger aura, denser sparkle, and up to ~1.9× shell output. You have the ginger. You do not have the Legendary ginger. That gap never closes.

04

Sets that pay off

Cats belong to themed sets. Complete one and you unlock a decoration and a set-exclusive cat you can't coax any other way — a second, slower collection layer stacked on the first.

05

Cats with something to do

Idle production, dramatised. Every station is a little loop of motion and sound — the part you leave running because it's nice to look at. This is where Cats & Soup actually spends its charm.

06

Cats with a story

A handful of named, one-of-a-kind cats — some always chaseable, some only around in the rain, or at night, or during an event. They give the cove lore and give returning players a headline to come back for.

07

The art follows the Midknight bible

This page prototypes the systems. The look is owned by the mascot bible — chunky-loaf proportions, warm-black / cool-neutral-white palette, deadpan register. Midknight (below) is rendered to that spec; the eleven collectible coats here are placeholder art to be redrawn the same way.

  1. Midknight is the starter cat & the mascot. Guaranteed on first run, never leaves, never enters the gacha.
  2. Every coat gets the loaf silhouette — head merged into the mass, ruff the widest point, no dark outline.
  3. Warm black, cool-neutral white shadow, one gold, one pink. The bible's colour rule, used everywhere.
  4. Deadpan register. Low energy by default; big reactions are rare and snap back fast.
  5. Rim light + contact shadow ground every cat in the same light.
  6. Hand-illustrated + rigged for shipping — this canvas is the concept, not the final frame.
08

Build order

Cheapest and highest-impact first. Tags mark rough effort.

  1. Contact shadows + one warm light pool + light-rim on every cat.half a day The whole-scene glow-up.
  2. The reveal.medium Anticipation → flip → rarity burst → meow. The single most-repeated moment in the game — it has to feel great.
  3. Rarity frames + auras + the pity timer.cheap Visual language for ★1–4, plus the guarantee that keeps a bad streak from feeling unfair.
  4. Idle secondary motion & emote bubbles.cheap Blink, ear/tail, plant sway; heart / zzz / note / sparkle bubbles.
  5. Procedural audio layer.medium Ambient bed (rain, stream, chimes) + synthesized meow / purr / chime. No sound files. Starts with a voice-tuning demo.
  6. Sets & the Catdex screen.medium Two stacked collection layers with milestone rewards.
  7. Activity stations.medium Fishing dock, catmint garden, bakery — each a motion + sound loop.
  8. Day/night grade, then accessories, then photo→clip capture.medium Polish and the share loop.
