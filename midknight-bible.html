01

Who he is

A grumpy-looking softie. The eyebrows say no. The belly-up sleeping says otherwise.

Midknight is the player's first cat and never leaves — the anchor of the cove and the character on the icon. His whole comedy is the gap between the deadpan face and the total mush underneath: he sighs when you pet him, then leans in; he judges the new cat, then falls asleep on it.

In the sleepy / huggable register he's the low-energy end: he loafs, he slow-blinks, he makes biscuits, he flops belly-up in a sunbeam. Big reactions are rare and therefore worth a lot — a wide-eyed Midknight is a screenshot.

He's built to merchandise: the silhouette works as a sticker, a plush, an app icon at 48 px. The white ruff and gold eyes carry him even when the black goes to pure shadow.

02

Marking map

Eight things make him Midknight and not a generic tuxedo. Render it however the illustration calls for — these eight stay, and stay asymmetric.

1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
  1. Black cap & mask. Solid black over crown, ears and back. Comes to a soft point between the brows — that point is what makes him look concerned.
  2. Lynx-tipped ears. Longhair furnishings: pale wisps inside, a couple of dark hairs past the tip. Tall, set fairly wide.
  3. Forehead freckle. A small scatter of white hairs on the black forehead, off-centre to his left. The single most identifying mark — never centre it, never drop it.
  4. Nose-bridge wedge. Black runs down the bridge from the mask point to a dark nose, splitting the white muzzle into two lobes.
  5. Chin dot (the "goatee"). A small round black patch on the white chin, just under the mouth. Rare on tuxedos — keep it.
  6. Gold eyes, low lids. Round amber eyes carried at ~60% open by default. The half-lid is the deadpan.
  7. Mitten paws. Front paws fully white, blunt and round, planted forward past the floof in the loaf.
  8. One back-leg spot. A single stray black smudge on a white hind leg. Small, easily missed, load-bearing for "hand-made, not generated."
Also fixed: the enormous white ruff (his widest point), the plume tail, prominent white whiskers and brow-whiskers, warm-black fur (never #000), cool-neutral shadow on the white.
03

Proportions

The loaf is the hero pose and the construction everything else derives from — a rounded triangle, base ≈ 1.5× the height, head merged into the mass rather than stacked on top.

  • Silhouette Rounded isosceles triangle. Flat-ish base, fluff pooled. Widest at the ruff, ~40% up.
  • Head : body Head width ≈ 0.62 of base width. No neck in the loaf — cheek floof meets ruff.
  • Eyeline Low — ~46% up the head, not centred. Big cats read younger with low eyes.
  • Eye size ~0.19 of head width each, ~1.1 eye-widths apart.
  • Ears Height ≈ 0.55 of head height, set wide with a shallow dip between.
  • Paws Two rounded blocks, together ~0.4 of base width, poking just past the front edge.
  • Line weight No outline. A warm rim on the top-left edge; forms read by value, not stroke.

07

Rig sheet

Built for a 2D skeletal rig (Spine / DragonBones) or Live2D — a breathing idle that never fully stops, layered so the deadpan can crack for a beat and settle back.

  1. Layer order (back → front): tail plume · far ear · back mass · haunches · ruff-back · head-black · face-white · muzzle + nose · eyes (sclera / iris / pupil / catchlight) · brow-whiskers · ruff-front · front legs + mittens · near ear · whiskers · rim-light overlay.
  2. Breathing idle: body mass scaleY ±1.5%, 0.9 s ease-in-out, ruff lags 2 frames. Always on.
  3. Blink: full close over 5 frames, hold 1, open over 4. Fire every 4–7 s, random. Slow-blink = 3× duration, used as the affection beat.
  4. Ears: independent bones, ±6° idle sway + occasional single-ear flick. Flatten to "annoyed" as one pose.
  5. Tail: 4-bone chain, base to tip, sine offset. Tip-only twitch on the deadpan; full slow curl on content.
  6. Whiskers: 3 bones each side, heavy droop, drift with head and breath. They sell the weight.
  7. Deform zones (panel): purple = squash/stretch on breath · gold = free-form face deform · green = bone chains.
  8. Reaction budget: 90% of the time he's in idle+blink. Big reactions resolve back to deadpan within 1.5 s — the snap-back is the joke.
08

Colour

One rule holds it together: the black is warm, the white's shadow is cool-neutral, there's one gold and one pink. Values below are the daylight grade; lamplight pushes everything warmer and drops the whites ~8%.

09

Do & don't

Do

  • Keep the half-lidded eyes — the deadpan is the brand.
  • Let the black go to warm shadow; never flatten to a silhouette.
  • Keep the freckle and chin dot asymmetric and slightly messy.
  • Make the ruff the widest, softest thing about him.
  • Give big reactions a fast snap-back to deadpan.
  • Draw whiskers heavy — they carry his weight and age.

Don't

  • Don't give him big round anime eyes — he squints.
  • Don't centre the forehead freckle or tidy it into a heart.
  • Don't use pure #000 — he dies at icon size.
  • Don't slim him down; the chonk is the hug.
  • Don't make him bouncy or hyper — that's a different cat.
  • Don't lose the chin dot in the black nose. They're separate.
