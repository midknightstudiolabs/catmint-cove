Catmint Café — emblem directions

Three original takes on a round café badge (no Starbucks artwork — same genre, our own cat). Each shown large, then at button size and favicon size, in the game's warm palette. Pick a direction and I'll build it into the picker header, the OPEN sign, the result screen, and the festival card.

A · The Roundel

The full café-crest read: wordmark wrapping the ring, cat face dead centre, a curl of steam on the brow. Most "coffee shop on the corner". Holds up as a stamp on signage and merch; the text disappears at tiny sizes but the face still reads.

B · Cat & Cup

A cat peering over a coffee cup, steam curling up as a catmint sprig — the two things the game is about, in one picture. Warmer and more characterful, less "chain". The cup shape alone works as the tiny icon.

cosiest / most on-brand for the game

C · The Cup-Cat

The coffee cup and the cat are the same shape — ears on the cup, handle kept, whiskers double as steam. Boldest and most "logo": one flat mark, terracotta + cream, reads perfectly at 16px. Least literal, most memorable.

most iconic / scales smallest
