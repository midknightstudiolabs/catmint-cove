Three directions

Same silhouette formula, three different heads/eyes/shading. All still cheap to draw per-frame — nothing here needs sprite art.

Baseline

Current (live game)

  • Realistic-ish round iris + pupil + lash line (deadpan half-lid)
  • Diagonal sticker-shading gradient, cool base
  • Pointy-ish ear, faint blush
Option A

Big Head, Bean Eyes

  • Head ~25% bigger, body a touch smaller → toddler proportions
  • Almond eyes with a coloured iris + vertical slit pupil — pointed cat ears, not a rounded teddy scoop
  • Flat two-tone fill instead of a gradient — reads clean at small sizes
Option B

Painterly Plush

  • Head modestly bigger, body rounder/chunkier, soft all-around outline
  • Keeps coloured iris + highlight, drops the lash line, softer eyelid
  • Airbrushed radial shading (warm top, soft shadow low) + always-on blush
Closest match to the festival concept art is a blend: Option A's proportions (the big head is what reads "cozy" from a distance) + Option B's shading (flat two-tone can look plasticky; the soft airbrush keeps the plush-toy feel). The crowd preview below renders that blend as Option C.

Crowd preview — Option C (A's head + B's shading)

Six cats in a row, mixed coats, sitting — the shape of the tug-of-war lineup without building the rope rig.

If this direction lands, the real change is small: swap the numbers in poseParams(), simplify drawEye(), and replace the diagonal silShadeGrad with a soft radial one. Everything else — coats, patterns, accessories, animation — keeps working as-is.
