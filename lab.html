Festival games in progress

Two new activities for the Cove Festival, still being built and balanced. They're not in the game yet — play them here and tell us what feels good. Best on a phone, held upright.

Tug of Paws

Playtest

Five cats on a rope against a visiting cove. Work the paw-spin, brace, and heave — bigger cats pull harder, and a little luck decides the rest. Whoever loses gets pulled toward the water.

Take the rope

Catmint Café

Prototype

You're the manager; your staff are cats. Three of them run a pop-up café on the festival grounds — barista, server, host. The only lever you pull is the shift change: who's on which job when the queue backs up.
