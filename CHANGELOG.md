# Changelog

Player-facing changes, newest first. Version line is the store build the
changes first shipped in.

---

## 2026-09-10 — iOS 1.0 (17) · Android 1.0.20

### Festival music
- The Cove Festival grounds have their own soundtrack now — a warm, cosy
  reworking of a real fairground track, cut into three moods that follow what
  you're doing: a light groove on the pick-an-activity screen, a faster,
  driving cut once a Race is on, and the sleepy breakdown under Volleyball
  with the wave wash and gulls layered over it. Starting an activity
  crossfades between them, so the music *moves* instead of cutting. The old
  synthesised calliope is kept only as a fallback.
- The cats on the festival landing screen bounce and shuffle in time with the
  music — dancing, full of energy.

### Cats
- Idle, sitting, and fishing cats stay planted on the ground now, instead of
  gently hovering over their own shadow.
- Cats do a little happy hop when they're in a good mood, wander over to ask
  for a fuss, and wind down afterwards — often curling up to sleep once
  they've been petted.

### The cove
- **The lean-to bakery** — a third restoration project at the far edge of the
  cove. Get the cold clay oven warm again and cats will bake there: kneading,
  shaping, and carrying warm buns round to share. Opens after the woodland
  path, and makes room for more cats.
- Two cats will chase each other around the meadow, zoomies-style, then flop
  down together as friends.
- Cats are solid against everything you place now — bowls, beds, stations —
  no more walking through the furniture.
- Thinned the meadow grass so the cove reads a little cleaner.

### Sound
- **New Sound panel** — tap the speaker button for separate volume sliders for
  cat voices, cove ambience, and music, plus an all-sound toggle. A quick
  double-tap still mutes everything at once.
- **iOS:** fixed audio staying silent after an interruption (a phone call,
  screen lock, Control Center) — it recovers on its own now.

### Catmint Cove Cares
- A short note in the game about the studio's intention to give back as
  Catmint Cove grows. It asks nothing of you — there's no donation flow and
  buying a pack is never a donation.

### Fixes & polish
- **App icon:** removed some stale artwork that was causing the wrong icon to
  ship on a few builds — the intended sitting-cat icon is correct now.
- **Arrange mode:** the controls bar no longer slides off-screen, and moving
  things around is easier on a phone.
- **Cove Volleyball:** rewards rebalanced to line up with the Race, and the
  result now reflects how well you actually played.
- The festival chooser fits on screen without a scrollbar.
- The tutorial mentions the festival is on its way, with its own beat
  ("Save the date").
- The app version is shown on the Credits screen.
