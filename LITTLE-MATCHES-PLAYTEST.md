# Little Matches — GitHub playtest

Open Activities > Little Matches. All 30 pages are selectable during this web test; this is not the final sequential unlock policy.

- Pick your resident companion and one free favour.
- No timer, lives, shell charge or currency reward.
- Match identical coats or Cove objects. Pairs add details to the side scene.
- Chapters 10/20/30 keep a cushion/flowers/lantern in the activity nook. Cushion colour can be changed after chapter one.
- First page and chapter completions add one Journal keepsake each.
- Unfinished deck, matches, turns and favour usage survive reload. Temporary reveals close when you leave.
- Cards never shuffle during a round; cats do not obstruct them.

Test pages 1, 10, 20 and 30; each favour; closing mid-round; refreshing; rotation; and chapter replay without duplicate keepsakes. Feedback priorities: card readability, usefulness of favours, whether the nook progression feels worthwhile, and whether later rounds remain relaxing.

Native iOS/Android packaging was not updated. No monetary reward changes. Uses existing drawn Cove art with short companion reactions; bespoke cat animation is not part of this first playtest.
