# Little Matches — reward progression playtest

Open Activities > Little Matches. Complete levels sequentially; replay selection unlocks once all 30 are complete. Existing save gaps are preserved and filled in order. An existing unfinished round can still be resumed.

- Pick a resident companion and one free favour. No timer, lives, entry fee, or currency payout.
- Match identical coats and Cove objects. The companion stage contains only your cat, with hops on matches, grooming after repeated mismatches or thinking time, and sleep after a pause.
- Reward accessories unlock at 5/10/15/20/25/30 for all resident cats. Preview locked rewards without equipping or spending. Earned rewards can be worn from the reveal or Dress-up.
- The progress trail shows the current five-level stretch. The collection shows all six rewards.
- Unfinished decks, matches, turns, and favour use persist. Leaving after the first Little peek selection consumes that favour; Second look needs a previous unmatched pair.
- Milestones enter the Journal once. No repeat farming or duplicates. Finish all 30 for Little Lion, the congratulations message, and next-season teaser.
- The opening still introduces pictures then visibly shuffles; positions stay fixed afterward.

Check small phones and landscape, all six outfit fits, changed companion names, opening/closing during animation, equipping and reloading, and reduced motion. Native iOS/Android packages have not been updated. Browser automation covers all 30 levels; real-device gameplay feedback is still needed.
