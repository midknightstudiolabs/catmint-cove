Cat tree — how it could look

Same flat sticker style as the bowls, beds and the scratch pad — wood posts, sisal wrap, felt platforms, the game's terracotta / cream / warm-wood palette. A cat loafs on the top perch (new "perched up high" spot). Three shapes to pick from.

A · The simple perch

One post, one round top platform, a dangling felt ball. Smallest footprint — reads clearly at any size. +1 capacity.

B · Two-tier

A mid step and a top perch, offset posts. A cat can nap on the lower one while another watches from the top. +2 capacity.

C · Perch + cubby

An open box cats curl up inside, a platform on top. The cosiest — closest to the cat cottage but vertical. +2 capacity.

These are quick vector mocks in the game's style, not final art. Pick a shape (or mix — "B but with C's cubby") and I'll draw it properly in drawAttractor, add the Comforts shop entry, the type, and a "perched up high" cat state (climb up, loaf on top, survey the cove, hop down).
