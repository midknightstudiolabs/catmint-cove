Privacy Policy — Catmint Cove

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Catmint Cove (“the app”, “the game”) is made by Midknight Studio Labs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is deliberately short because the app is deliberately simple.

The short version

  • No account, sign-up or login is needed to play. The whole game is playable straight away. Cove Friends is optional and uses a guest identity: no email, no password.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. It is saved in the app’s local storage. We never see it. Only if you choose to join Cove Friends is a small, friends-only card of your cats and café shared (see below).
  • We do not run ads and we do not use any tracking SDKs. Optional, anonymous feature counts are sent only if you turn on “Help improve the Cove”.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never share it for advertising.
  • Data leaves your device only for (1) in-app purchases, handled by the app store and our billing provider, (2) Cove Friends, if you join, and (3) optional feature counts, if you opt in.

Game data

Your cove — its name, your cats, your progress, your settings — is stored only on your device using standard on-device storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently removes it. We provide no cloud backup at this time, so an uninstall loses your progress.

Cove Friends (optional)

Cove Friends lets you visit friends, send hearts, gifts and cards, and play friendly matches. It is off until you tap Join Cove Friends. Joining creates a guest identity (no email, no password) and a 12-character friend code, and stores the following on our hosted database (Supabase):

  • the cove name you choose and your friend code;
  • a small “cove card”: the names, looks and stars of up to six cats, your Cove Guardian, and your café’s name, stage, décor and served count;
  • your friend list, and the hearts, gifts and cards you send or receive (cards use fixed messages, never free text).

Only friends you have accepted can see your cove card. Your Shells balance, purchases and save file are never uploaded. Gifts move Shells between devices, with daily limits enforced by the server. You can remove or block anyone at any time. To have your Friends data deleted, contact us using the address below with your friend code.

In-app purchases

The app offers a few optional in-app purchases: cosmetic items, and an optional auto-renewing subscription, Midknight’s Blessing, that adds convenience features (such as Harvest all and Feed all) and a small Shell bonus. Nothing is required to play or finish the game. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. Your payment details are handled entirely by them and are never seen by us. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in your store account settings.

To deliver purchased items reliably across reinstalls and devices, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as our billing provider. When you make (or restore) a purchase, RevenueCat receives and stores:

  • a randomly generated app user ID (an anonymous identifier — not your name, email, or Google account), and
  • your purchase history for this app (which items you bought, and when).

RevenueCat processes this to tell the app which items you own. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, Google account, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising ID from this app. See RevenueCat’s own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not share it with any other third party.

Children

Catmint Cove is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app has no social features, no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no behavioural advertising.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without purchasing. The full core experience is available for free; purchases are entirely optional.
  • Delete your data. Your game data: uninstall the app or clear its storage. Purchase records held by RevenueCat: email us at the address below and we will delete them.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at the same URL.
