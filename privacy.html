Privacy Policy — Catmint Cove

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Catmint Cove (“the app”, “the game”) is made by Midknight Studio Labs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is deliberately short because the app is deliberately simple.

The short version

  • No account. No sign-up. No login. The whole game is playable straight away.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. It is saved in the app’s local storage. We never see it, and it is never uploaded to us.
  • We do not run ads and we do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what’s needed to process an in-app purchase, handled by Google Play and our billing provider (see below).

Game data

Your cove — its name, your cats, your progress, your settings — is stored only on your device using standard on-device storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently removes it. We provide no cloud backup at this time, so an uninstall loses your progress.

In-app purchases

The app offers a few optional, cosmetic-only in-app purchases (decorative items — they have no effect on gameplay or progress). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Your payment details are handled entirely by Google and are never seen by us.

To deliver purchased items reliably across reinstalls and devices, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as our billing provider. When you make (or restore) a purchase, RevenueCat receives and stores:

  • a randomly generated app user ID (an anonymous identifier — not your name, email, or Google account), and
  • your purchase history for this app (which items you bought, and when).

RevenueCat processes this to tell the app which items you own. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, Google account, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising ID from this app. See RevenueCat’s own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not share it with any other third party.

Children

Catmint Cove is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app has no social features, no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no behavioural advertising.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without purchasing. The full core experience is available for free; purchases are entirely optional.
  • Delete your data. Your game data: uninstall the app or clear its storage. Purchase records held by RevenueCat: email us at the address below and we will delete them.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at the same URL.
