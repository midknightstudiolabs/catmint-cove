# Catmint Cove 3.4.4

Grow ingredients in the redesigned Cove Garden and serve cats in a simpler Catmint Café. Café orders run automatically, with progress from taking an order through brewing, cooking and serving. Tap to help, unlock and name recipes, and welcome customers ordering a drink and snack together.

- Garden planting, watering, harvesting and café stock in one view.
- Choose your café companion and see them inside and outside.
- Clearer help, first-time guidance and small-screen tutorial placement.
- Updated early visitor introduction and loading-screen cat audio.

## Device checks
- Update without losing the Cove, recipes, inventory or purchases.
- Watch at least three customers without tapping; check a mixed order and optional boost.
- Run out of ingredients, harvest or restock, then reopen.
- Complete and skip the opening guide on a small phone; check cat arrivals remain visible.
- Check Garden motion, audio/mute, Friends recovery and purchase restore on device.

Automated engine and introductory-flow checks do not replace these device checks.
