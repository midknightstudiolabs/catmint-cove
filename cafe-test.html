Catmint Café

Pop-up café

Your cats run the festival café. You don't take orders or make coffee — you decide who works where.

Coffee backing up? Queue at the door? Swap a cat onto that job. They'll walk over and take it — which is the only cost. You can't stop a cat being a cat.

☕ Barista — makes the drinks & food
🍰 Server — takes orders, delivers them
🎀 Host — greets & seats arrivals
