# Catmint Cove Neo — launch creative kit

## Positioning
A little seaside home. A few peculiar cats. Somehow, they become yours.

Free companion game for cat lovers and cozy-game players who want personal routines, gentle humor and a place to return to. Support the studio through optional expression and decoration, not urgency.

## Store/landing draft
Make a little home for some very particular cats.

Meet Midknight, welcome new faces, discover their favorite things, and keep the small moments that make them yours. Decorate your coastal home, join a friendly festival, or settle into the rainy retreat. Your cats are looked after while you are away.

Build a Cove worth coming home to.

Do not publish platform availability or real payment claims until the signed store build and billing are verified. The browser collections are previews.

## Three real-gameplay creative briefs
1. Recognition, 10–15 seconds. Show a cat ignoring the purchased bed and choosing another spot. Caption: “Bought the bed. The cat had other plans.” End on its name and the Cove. Only use a behavior captured in the actual game.
2. Attachment, 15–20 seconds. Show two actual friends together, open the personal story and reveal their friendship. Caption: “Somewhere along the way, they became your little family.” No fabricated relationship timeline.
3. Expression, 12–18 seconds. Show one corner before/after arrangement, then its resident settling there. Caption: “A little corner. Entirely yours.” Use existing available decor, not unbuilt paid collections.

## Release post draft
The little things stay.

Neo now has personal cat stories, a journal for your memories, simpler navigation, and kinder care while you are away. Come meet the cats, find their favorite things, and make a little seaside home your own.

Play: https://midknightstudiolabs.github.io/catmint-cove-neo/

## Measurement
Compare creatives by first-session completion, voluntary next-day returns and meaningful cat interactions, not just clicks. Record acquisition source only through an explicitly chosen analytics/privacy setup. No paid ad campaign has been launched.

## Product pricing tests
Keep current Welcome / Sparkle / Founder pricing as hypotheses. Test perceived value with exact contents and real previews. A future coordinated room collection must contain enough finished, usable assets to justify its price. No limited-time pressure, affection sales, or purchased rarity odds.

Illustrative planning equation: monthly active users × purchaser fraction × average purchaser spend = gross revenue before platform fees, taxes, refunds, content production and acquisition. Validate inputs before buying traffic.
